# Support

Player instructions are available in [English](docs/guide.md) and [Russian](docs/guide.ru.md). Start there for game-folder detection, GameBanana login, conflicts, profile keys, updates and removal.

For a bug, question or feature request, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/AlexUner/pocket-deadlock-mod-manager/issues/new/choose). English and Russian reports are welcome. Support is provided as time allows; no response-time guarantee is offered.

Include your app version, Windows version, steps and exact error. For mod-specific problems, add the public source URL and chosen archive variant. Redact personal paths and account information in screenshots. Do not attach the complete local data directory.

- Antivirus detection: [verification and reporting](docs/antivirus.md).
- Exploitable security issue: [private reporting policy](SECURITY.md).
- Contributing a fix: [contributor guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Mod authors handle mod compatibility and usage questions specific to their files. PocketDeadlock support cannot restore content removed by a source website or grant access to account-restricted listings.
